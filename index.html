Publications

Multi-Tool Robotics Enables In-Situ Sample Manipulation for Time-Resolved Synchrotron Measurements

Aditya Bondada, Elizabeth M. Wall, Eric Yuan Xiao, Quinn C. Burlingame, Yueh-Lin Loo, Esther H. R. Tsai, Ruipeng Li

Under review at Digital Discovery (Royal Society of Chemistry), 2026 arXiv:2608.19280 cs.RO

A modular multi-tool robot that handles and processes samples inside the experimental hutch during continuous X-ray data collection, revealing transient in-situ dynamics in perovskite thin films that radiation-safety protocols previously made inaccessible.

Research & Selected Projects

Extensible Robotic Beamline Scientist (EROBS)

Brookhaven National Laboratory, NSLS-II · deployed at CMS beamline

A hardware-agnostic ROS2 motion planning stack integrated with Bluesky, the control software used across national laboratories. Orchestrates dynamic tool changing — vacuum, gripper, pipettor — so a single robot can serve varied experiments under beamline safety constraints.

Project page

Robotic Pipette End Effector

Brookhaven National Laboratory, NSLS-II

A microcontroller-driven precision liquid-handling end effector using RS485 communication, achieving 5 µL ±0.5 µL accuracy on a UR5e with quick-swap tooling.

Project page Poster

Autonomous UAV Navigation & SLAM

Stony Brook University · 2025

A lightweight SLAM pipeline for a resource-constrained UAV, fusing IMU with sparse four-point LiDAR for real-time obstacle mapping. D* Lite planning cut re-planning time 20% against A*, with minimum-snap trajectories feeding a cascaded controller.

Project page

Cobot Trajectory Planning

Stony Brook University · 2025

Forward and inverse kinematics for an AUBO i5 from its URDF, with Jacobian-based velocity control and Monte Carlo workspace estimation to characterize kinematic singularities.
