Robotic Pipette End Effector

In Service

High-precision robotic liquid handling system achieving 5μL accuracy for scientific research applications - Developed Summer 2025 at Brookhaven National Laboratory

Overview

Industrial arms move precisely but cannot handle liquids. This end effector gives a UR5e microliter pipetting: two 3D-printed actuators driven over RS485, with a ROS2 driver on top. It hits 5 µL ±0.5 µL, takes standard laboratory pipettes, and mounts on the arm’s existing tool exchanger. Built at NSLS-II so a robot could run liquid-handling steps inside an active beamline hutch.

Design

Dual actuators

One actuator drives the plunger with position feedback, a second ejects the tip with a force limit. Stepper motors give repeatable travel, which is what the volume accuracy actually depends on.

Quick-swap holder

Pipettes change without stopping the workflow. The holder locks the tool during motion and repeats its alignment on re-seating, so accuracy survives a swap.

Integration

RS485 to the microcontroller, exposed to ROS2 as an end effector the motion stack can call. It shares the UR5e tool exchanger with the gripper and vacuum tools.
